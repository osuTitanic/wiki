# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Titanic! (also referred to as "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our service at https://osu.titanic.sh.

By using the service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

### Registration Data

Registering means that we create a unique account for you to access our service.  
When you register for an account, we collect the following information:

- **Username** - your chosen username for identification on the service
- **Email address** - Used for account verification, communication, and security notifications
- **Password** - Stored securely using modern hashing algorithms for account security
- **IP address** - Collected automatically upon registration and during use of the service for security purposes
- **Country** - Used to display a country flag on your profile (can be changed upon request)

### Optional Data

You can voluntarily provide additional information on your public user profile, such as:

- **your social media presence**
- **your forum signature**
- **your interests**
- **your avatar**
- **your location**

All information provided in these fields is user-submitted content. Users have full control over what they choose to share, and the information is not required to be accurate or reflect their real-life circumstances. For example, the location field can contain arbitrary information and should not be interpreted as the user's actual geographical location.

All the above fields are publicly visible and can be edited or removed at any time through the [profile settings page](https://osu.titanic.sh/account/profile).

### Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the service.

Usage Data may include information such as your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, browser version, the pages of our service that you visit, the time and date of your visit and other diagnostic data.

This information is generally processed only for the duration of a single request and is not permanently stored or retained after the request has been completed.

An exception applies to website and in-game authentication, where certain information may be recorded and retained for security purposes, such as preventing unauthorized access and investigating suspicious login activity.

### In-game activity

When you access our service through the osu! client, we collect additional information to provide the service and maintain security. Each login through the osu! client is recorded for security purposes, including the current date and time, IP address, and device information. You can view your login history in your [account security settings](https://osu.titanic.sh/account/security) to monitor access to your account and identify any unauthorized login attempts.

We also collect user-generated content that you create and submit through the osu! client. This includes gameplay scores and replays, chat messages, beatmap submissions, beatmap comments and ratings, as well as user profile information and statistics. This data is essential to provide the core functionality of the service and to maintain the community features that make the platform functional.

### Forum Activity

Our service includes a forum where users can participate in community discussions. When you use the forum, we collect the content you submit, including forum posts, replies, and any other contributions you make to forum threads. This content is publicly visible to other users of the service and is associated with your account username. Forum posts are stored to maintain the continuity of discussions and preserve the community knowledge base. Please be mindful that any information you share in forum posts will be publicly accessible and should not include sensitive personal information.

### Cookies

Cookies are small files that are placed on your computer, mobile device or any other device by a website. Cookies can be "Persistent" or "Session" Cookies. Persistent Cookies remain on your personal computer or mobile device when you go offline, while Session Cookies are deleted as soon as you close your web browser. You can learn more about cookies on the [website article by TermsFeed](https://www.termsfeed.com/blog/cookies/).

We use both Session and Persistent Cookies for the purposes set out below:

#### Essential Cookies

Purpose: These Cookies are essential to provide you with services available through the website and to enable you to use some of its features. They help to authenticate users and prevent fraudulent use of user accounts. Without these Cookies, the services that you have asked for cannot be provided, and we only use these Cookies to provide you with those services.

#### Functionality Cookies

Purpose: These Cookies allow us to remember choices you make when you use the website, such as remembering your login details. The purpose of these Cookies is to provide you with a more personal experience and to avoid you having to re-enter your preferences every time you use the website.

## Your Data Protection Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Access** - You can access and review your personal information through your [account settings](https://osu.titanic.sh/account/security)
- **Correction** - You can update or correct your information at any time through your [account settings](https://osu.titanic.sh/account/profile)
- **Right to Be Forgotten** - You have the right to request the erasure of your personal data
- **Restriction** - You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@titanic.sh or use the tools available in your [account settings](https://osu.titanic.sh/account/profile).  
Please note that we may need to retain certain information when we have a legal obligation to do so.

As we have a strict one-account-per-user policy to maintain fair leaderboards, after account erasure you may not be able to return to our service.  
To enforce this, we will also maintain the device identifiers as mentioned in "In-game activity", even after account erasure. This is done so as a legitimate interest as [outlined in the GDPR](https://www.privacy-regulation.eu/en/article-6-lawfulness-of-processing-GDPR.htm) in order to enforce our rules. These identifiers are one-way hashed and stored independently of any account information and cannot be used to determine the identity of a deleted account or recover any information previously associated with it. They are used exclusively to detect whether a newly registered account matches identifiers previously associated with a deleted account.
