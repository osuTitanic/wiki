# Koishi's Titanic Score Camp

**Koishi's Titanic Score Camp** (***TSC***) was an international osu! ranked score competition hosted by [KoishiTheKoishi](https://osu.titanic.sh/u/1022) and [Cloudpaw](https://osu.titanic.sh/u/2302).

## Schedule

| Event | Timestamp |
| --: | :-- |
| Registrations open | 2026-05-25 |
| Tracking starts | 2026-07-11, 15:00 UTC |
| Tracking ends | 2026-07-31, 15:00 UTC |

## Prizes

Koishi's Titanic Score Camp offered a $225 USD cash prize pool.

| Placing | Prizes |
| :-: | :-- |
| 1st | $150, unique profile badge |
| 2nd | $75, unique profile badge |
| 3rd | $50, unique profile badge |

The prize pool was sponsored by various community members:

- [puffle osu](https://osu.titanic.sh/u/3847): $100
- [Levi](https://osu.titanic.sh/u/2): $50
- [skrillex](https://osu.titanic.sh/u/1069): $50
- [BlakeBelladonna](https://osu.titanic.sh/u/2453): $25

All participants were given a profile stamp showing their placing and ranked score gained during the event.
